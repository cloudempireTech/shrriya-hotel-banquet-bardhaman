A PRIME LANDMARK VENUE

Rooted at Bardhaman's Arterial Junction

Conceived to provide Burdwan with an international-grade banquet and hospitality landmark, SHRRIYA HOTEL & BANQUET stands proudly at Alisha Bus Stand junction. With zero congestive town bottlenecks, outstation relatives from Kolkata, Durgapur, Asansol, and rural Bengal can drive straight up to the venue via Grand Trunk Road and National Highway 19.

Our premises feature a seamless integration of grand ceremonial banquet halls, private bridal makeup suites, expansive dining zones, and air-conditioned hotel suites to lodge outstation families with complete peace of mind.
